## Read this first

1. **The Maccabi file has no hospital columns, no co-pay columns and no subspecialty for foot & ankle.** Its columns are: title, first name, last name, license, medical field, specialization (the only orthopedic subspecialty listed is hand surgery), active, location count, and towns 1–4. **None of the co-pays below come from the file.** Every Sheli/Zahav amount has to be looked up per procedure in the online directory (serguide.maccabi4u.co.il) or by calling the clinic. The doctor page I opened in serguide only shows the *visit* fee (quarterly co-pay), not the surgery co-pay.
2. **Rule from the Aug-2026 regulations:** Zahav §6: you can choose a private surgeon in a private hospital only if *both* the surgeon and the hospital are in agreement with Maccabi *for that procedure*. The co-pay is derived from the cost of the operation, minimum ₪300. Sheli §34: with an in-agreement surgeon you get an extra discount of up to 50% off the Zahav co-pay. In some cases the Sheli and Zahav co-pays are the same, and never below ₪300. Both have a 12-month waiting period. Sources: maccabi_sheli_regulations.pdf, maccabi_zahav_regulations.pdf on maccabi4u.co.il.
3. **The file looks incomplete, or it is a narrower list than 'all surgeon-choice surgeons'.** It has only 149 orthopedists. Only 1 of the 9 surgeons on Raphael's MICA list (Feldbrin) is in it. Bermant's own site says he works in agreement with Maccabi, yet he is not in it. Before relying on an 'N', confirm in serguide or with Maccabi (*3555).
4. Evidence scale: **strong** = the surgeon is first or senior author of a MIS-HV outcome or technique paper, or has their own MIS-HV page *plus* an independent MIS listing. **moderate** = own MIS-HV page, or listed on a hospital MIS/MICA page, or co-author of MIS-HV papers. **weak** = a generic 'minimally invasive foot & ankle' claim with nothing bunion-specific. **none** = foot & ankle surgeon, no MIS found. Being a foot & ankle surgeon was never counted as MIS evidence.

- **In the Maccabi file and some MIS evidence:** Feldbrin (Wolfson F&A head; on Raphael's MICA list, hospital-level evidence only). Yaron Brin (Hadera/Kfar Saba; co-author of 3 MICA papers, but his own MIS bunion practice isn't confirmed). Snir Heller (2010 co-author only; weak). Salman (Haifa/Afula; weak, his MIS claim is about toes). Eidelman (Haifa; pediatric percutaneous only).
- **Haifa reality:** no Haifa surgeon has strong MIS-bunion evidence. The best is Ben Zvi (Carmel): explicitly MIS for HV, but with temporary K-wires and ~6 weeks of restrictions. That is an older-generation technique, not 3rd/4th-generation screw-fixed MICA, and he is not in the file. Margulis (Carmel) trained at Meir in 2008-09, Palmanovich's unit. That's worth asking him about directly, but it is not evidence.
- **Quote check, not just name check:** surgeon choice needs surgeon + hospital + procedure in agreement. Several MIS surgeons operate at Raphael (Tel Aviv). Ask Maccabi which hospital the agreement covers for *hallux valgus correction* with that surgeon.
